Six clouds worth knowing

Look up, find the height, and you can guess what the weather will do next.

CirrusAbove 6 km

Thin, feathery streaks made of ice crystals. When they thicken and spread, a warm front is often 12–24 hours away, so rain may follow.

Altostratus2–6 km

A grey veil that makes the sun look like it's behind frosted glass. It often arrives before steady rain or snow.

Cumulus0.5–2 km

Flat-bottomed cotton puffs. Small ones mean fair weather. If they keep growing upward by midday, showers are possible.

StratusBelow 2 km

A flat grey blanket with no shape. It brings gloom, mist or light drizzle. Fog is essentially stratus on the ground.

Nimbostratus0.5–3 km

Thick, dark and featureless. This is the cloud behind long, steady rain or snow lasting hours.

CumulonimbusBase 0.5–2 km, top 12 km+

The thunderstorm cloud. Strong rising air builds a tower that flattens into an anvil. Expect lightning, heavy rain, gusts and sometimes hail.
